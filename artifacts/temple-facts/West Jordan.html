West Jordan Utah Temple

Status
Announced, with the site being prepared for construction. Preliminary earthwork began in late 2025 and continued through spring 2026, and the temple site has been graded and staked, but ground has not been formally broken. No groundbreaking, open house, or dedication dates have been announced.

Location
West Jordan, Utah, a Salt Lake City suburb on the southwest side of the Salt Lake Valley. The temple will rise on a 16.1-acre site at approximately 7148 South, west of Highway 111, near the western edge of the city, at an elevation of 5,308 feet. The grounds will also hold a new meetinghouse with shared parking.

Temple Number
The first temple for the city of West Jordan and, when built, the sixth in Salt Lake County. At its announcement it became the 29th temple for Utah, counting temples operating, under renovation, under construction, or announced. Its number among the Church's dedicated temples will be fixed at its dedication.

Announced
April 7, 2024, by President Russell M. Nelson in the concluding session of the 194th Annual General Conference, one of 15 temples announced that day.

Architecture
A multi-story temple of approximately 85,000 square feet. The exterior rendering, released April 14, 2025, shows a single spire attached at one end of the building.

Construction Story

  • The site was released on April 22, 2024, two weeks after the announcement, in the same First Presidency release that placed the Lehi Utah and Tampa Florida temples.
  • Preliminary earthwork was visible at the site by late November 2025, and crews spent early 2026 grading and staking the property. A groundbreaking date will be announced once construction is ready to begin.

Trivia

  • It was announced the same day as the Lehi Utah Temple, and the two share the same plan on paper: each a multi-story temple of approximately 85,000 square feet with a new meetinghouse on the temple grounds.
  • The 15 temples announced on April 7, 2024 brought the Church's worldwide total of temples, built or announced, to 350.
  • It will be the westernmost of Salt Lake County's six temples, about 10 miles northwest of the Oquirrh Mountain Utah Temple in South Jordan.

Temple facts verified as of August 25, 2026.
