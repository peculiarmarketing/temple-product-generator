Cody Wyoming Temple

Status
Under construction. The public open house runs August 27 through September 12, 2026, excluding Sundays, and admission is free with no reservation required. Dedication is scheduled for Sunday, October 25, 2026.

Location
555 Temple View Lane, Cody, Wyoming. A 4.69-acre site northwest of Skyline Drive at Cody Canal, in the northwestern corner of the state near the Montana border, at an elevation of 5,134 feet with Heart Mountain on the horizon.

Temple Number
The third temple built in Wyoming, following Star Valley in 2016 and Casper in 2024. Its number among dedicated temples will be fixed at dedication.

Announced
October 3, 2021, by President Russell M. Nelson, at the October 2021 general conference, among thirteen temples announced that session.

Groundbreaking
September 27, 2024. Elder Steven R. Bangerter, First Counselor in the North America Central Area Presidency, presided and offered the site dedicatory prayer. Elder Kevin R. Duncan, Executive Director of the Temple Department, also attended.

Architecture
A single-story building of 9,950 square feet, 101 feet to the top of the spire. The exterior is precast concrete panels. A single attached central tower rises as a multi-tiered steeple capped with a gold pyramidal spire. The official exterior rendering was released March 27, 2023.

Ordinance Rooms
One instruction room, one sealing room, and one baptistry.

Construction Story

  • The site was announced in March 2023 as an empty field northwest of Skyline Drive, roughly seventeen months after the temple itself was announced.
  • Modular construction methods carried the project at unusual speed, with the exterior essentially finished about eighteen months after work began.
  • In his site dedicatory prayer, Elder Bangerter asked a blessing on the surrounding community rather than only on the Church membership, praying that neighbors would feel a spirit of peace and that the temple's presence would bring added joy, prosperity, beauty, and unity to their lives.

Symbolism & Design

  • The gold pyramidal spire replaces the angel Moroni statue found on many older temples, a choice increasingly common among the small single-story temples built in this era.
  • At 9,950 square feet, Cody is built to the scale of its community rather than to the scale of a regional temple. The single instruction room and single sealing room are the design's honest statement about who it is for.

Trivia

  • Nearly 67,000 Latter-day Saints live in Wyoming, meeting in more than 170 congregations, roughly one in every nine residents of the state.
  • Members in northwest Wyoming currently travel to the Billings Montana Temple. Cody will end that drive for them.
  • Cody is already home to the Historic Cody Mural and Museum, a Church meetinghouse whose domed ceiling carries a large mural depicting the Restoration and the westward migration. The chapel and visitors' center are open to the public during summer months.
  • Wyoming's pioneer connection is not incidental. Latter-day Saint companies crossed the state throughout the 1840s to 1860s on their way to the Salt Lake Valley.
