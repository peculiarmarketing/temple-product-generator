Bountiful Utah Temple

Location
Bountiful, Utah. The temple stands at 640 South Bountiful Boulevard, in the foothills of the east bench overlooking the Great Salt Lake, about nine miles north of Salt Lake City, at an elevation of 5,160 feet.

Temple Number
The 47th operating temple of the Church, the eighth in Utah, and the first in Davis County.

Announced
February 2, 1990, by a First Presidency letter to local leaders in Davis County: "We have now determined to undertake the construction of a temple on this site." The Church had announced the purchase of the hillside property for a possible temple on May 28, 1988.

Groundbreaking
May 2, 1992. President Ezra Taft Benson presided, with his counselors President Gordon B. Hinckley and President Thomas S. Monson participating. About 7,500 people attended on the site and 2,500 more watched by video.

Dedicated
January 8 through 14, 1995, by President Howard W. Hunter, in 28 sessions. President Hunter attended six of the sessions and spoke in four; Apostles read his dedicatory prayer in the remainder.

Architecture
A modern single-spire design by architect of record Allen B. Erekson, with Keith Stepan as project architect. The exterior is Bethel white granite quarried near Sharon, Vermont. The temple encloses 104,000 square feet and rises 176 feet to the angel Moroni statue atop its spire.

Ordinance Rooms
Four instruction rooms, eight sealing rooms, and a baptistry.

First Temple President
Harold C. Yancey, 1994 to 1997.

Construction Story

  • The groundbreaking crowd was so large that President Hinckley compared it to "the days of King Benjamin," when the people gathered around the temple by families.
  • Architect Allen B. Erekson stated the design goal plainly: "What we are trying to accomplish in the Bountiful Utah Temple is to have a level of elegance that doesn't distract from the Spirit."
  • During the public open house, November 5 through December 17, 1994, exactly 870,361 visitors toured the temple, assisted by some 45,000 volunteers.

Symbolism & Design

  • Exterior detailing represents the sun, moon, and stars, a reference to the scriptural descriptions of the three degrees of glory.
  • The art glass, created by Arthur Stern Studio of Oakland, California, carries a Jacob's Ladder motif of ascending detail, with beveled crystal that refracts sunlight into rainbow effects inside.
  • Interior colors deepen and lighten by floor: rich, warm tones on the lower levels give way to lighter, more elegant tones as one ascends.

Changes Over Time

  • Lightning struck the angel Moroni statue on May 22, 2016, and a replacement statue was set atop the spire on June 1, 2016.

Trivia

  • The 28 dedicatory sessions drew 201,655 attendees, reported as the largest attendance at any temple dedication in the history of the Church.
  • Bountiful was one of only two temples dedicated by President Howard W. Hunter, whose presidency lasted nine months; he died less than two months after the dedication.
  • The temple's floor plan was adapted for the Mount Timpanogos Utah Temple, dedicated the following year.

Temple facts verified as of August 19, 2026.
