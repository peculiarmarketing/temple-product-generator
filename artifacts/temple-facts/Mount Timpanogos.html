Mount Timpanogos Utah Temple

Location
American Fork, Utah, on nearly 17 acres at the base of Mount Timpanogos. The temple is named for the mountain behind it rather than for the city it stands in.

Temple Number
The 49th dedicated temple. It was the ninth in Utah and the second in Utah County.

Announced
October 3, 1992, by President Gordon B. Hinckley, then First Counselor in the First Presidency.

Groundbreaking
October 9, 1993. President Gordon B. Hinckley presided and offered the dedicatory prayer.

Dedicated
October 13 to 19, 1996, by President Gordon B. Hinckley, in 27 sessions. He presided over and spoke in 11 of them.

Architecture
Sierra white granite with art glass windows and bronze doors. 104,000 square feet, rising 190 feet to the top of the spire, with a single attached central spire and an angel Moroni statue.

Ordinance Rooms
Four instruction rooms, eight sealing rooms, one baptistry.

Construction Story

  • The floor plan was adapted from the Bountiful Utah Temple, so the two buildings look much alike apart from their spires.
  • About 12,000 people attended the groundbreaking in 1993. The location of the Madrid Spain Temple was announced at the same ceremony.
  • The angel Moroni statue was set in place on July 17, 1995, before an estimated 20,000 spectators.

Symbolism & Design

  • The exterior is faced in sierra white granite, with art glass windows and bronze doors.
  • The building sits at the foot of Mount Timpanogos, and takes its name from the peak rather than from any settlement.

Trivia

  • 679,217 people toured the temple during the open house, held August 6 through September 21, 1996.
  • An estimated 20,000 people came out to watch the angel Moroni statue be set in place, more than a year before the temple was dedicated.

Temple facts verified as of August 20, 2026.
