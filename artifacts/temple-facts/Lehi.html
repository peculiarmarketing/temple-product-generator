Lehi Utah Temple

Status
Announced and in design. Preliminary site work began in August 2025, but no groundbreaking ceremony has been scheduled, and no open house or dedication dates have been announced.

Location
Lehi, Utah. A 22.48-acre site northwest of the intersection of 3950 North and Center Street, in northern Utah County, in the technology corridor known as Silicon Slopes.

Temple Number
The 30th temple announced for Utah. Its number among dedicated temples will be fixed when it is dedicated.

Announced
April 7, 2024, by Russell M. Nelson at the close of the 194th Annual General Conference.

Architecture
A multi-story building of approximately 85,000 square feet with a single attached central spire. The exterior rendering was released February 24, 2025. Materials, room counts and interior details have not yet been published.

Construction Story

  • Work on the site started before the ceremony did. In August 2025 crews began running underground water, sewer and electrical service out to meet the city's existing utilities, and grading the Church's property, all of it classed as pre-groundbreaking work.
  • The temple is not going up alone. The plans for the site include a new meetinghouse alongside it.

Symbolism & Design

  • Only the exterior rendering has been released so far. The art glass, palette and local motifs that modern temples are designed around are normally published at media day, a few days before an open house opens, so those details are still some way off for Lehi.

Temple facts verified as of September 16, 2026.
