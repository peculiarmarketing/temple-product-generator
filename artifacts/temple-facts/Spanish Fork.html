Spanish Fork Utah Temple

Status
Announced and in the planning and design phase. Ground has not been broken, and no groundbreaking date has been announced. The Church confirmed the site on January 12, 2026, and the First Presidency released the official exterior rendering on July 13, 2026.

Location
Spanish Fork, Utah. The temple will rise on an 8.7-acre site at the corner of 100 South and 2550 East, at an elevation of 4,729 feet, in southern Utah Valley with Utah Lake to the northwest and the Wasatch Range to the east.

Temple Number
The 9th temple announced for Utah County and the 32nd for Utah, counting temples in operation, under renovation, under construction, or announced; no state has more. Its number among the Church's dedicated temples will be fixed at its dedication.

Announced
April 6, 2025, by President Russell M. Nelson during the 195th Annual General Conference, one of 15 temples announced that day.

Architecture
Plans call for a multistory temple of approximately 80,000 square feet with an accompanying ancillary building. The official rendering shows two attached end spires.

Trivia

  • Spanish Fork was the last temple President Russell M. Nelson announced. He named it in his final general conference address, and he died that September.
  • The announcement came exactly 148 years to the day after Utah's first temple, the St. George Utah Temple, was dedicated on April 6, 1877.
  • Latter-day Saints settled Spanish Fork in 1851. The city was home to about 50,000 residents when the temple was announced.

Temple facts verified as of August 25, 2026.
