CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Site acreage - Newsroom releases (T1) say 17.9 acres; T2 spec panel says 18.17 acres. T1 wins; published 17.9 acres.
Baptistry count - Church News Almanac (T1, drawn from 2022 reporting) says two baptistries; T2 spec panel (currently maintained) says one baptistry. Could not determine which reflects the final plan, so no baptistry count shipped; published only the four instruction rooms and four sealing rooms both tiers agree on.
Floor area - T1 says approximately 88,000 square feet; T2 gives 87,626. Not a real conflict (approximation vs precise figure); published the T1 approximation for batch consistency.
County approval date - T2 and court coverage say the council vote was November 8, 2023, after a hearing that ran late into the night; some outlets date it November 9. Published as "November 2023" to sidestep the day ambiguity.
