SOURCE LEDGER - Oakland California Temple

VERIFIED
[T1] Announced Jan 23, 1961 by David O. McKay; groundbreaking May 26, 1962 with McKay presiding alongside his First Presidency counselors; completed Sept 27, 1964; open house Oct 5-31, 1964 extended an additional week, about 347,000 visitors; dedicated Nov 17-19, 1964 by David O. McKay in six sessions with more than 120 general authorities and spouses attending; rededicated June 16, 2019 by Dallin H. Oaks in three sessions with Elder David A. Bednar - thechurchnews.com almanac
[T1] Second temple in California, making California the second state with more than one; granite relief panels 35 ft by 13 ft; interstake center built 1959 once served as the Golden State Warriors practice facility; front doors malfunctioned shortly after the 1964 dedication and patrons used a side entrance for years; waterfall removed in 1969 for irreparable leaks and restored in the renovation - thechurchnews.com almanac
[T1] Approximately 95,000 sq ft; five spires with a central tower at 170 ft; renovation of about 16 months beginning February 2018; open house May 11 - June 1, 2019; renovation materials: flooring stone from Greece, China and Georgia, primavera wood from South America for the celestial room doors, ribbon-striped mahogany from Africa in sealing rooms, custom bronze hardware of Indian brass, paints from California and Ohio, lighting designed in Minnesota, New York and Utah - thechurchnews.com, newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org
[T1] Architect Harold W. Burton (1888-1969) drew on a career of Church buildings and on 1960s design; the five-spire design points to Far Eastern sacred architecture including the Taj Mahal and Angkor Wat, chosen to reflect the diversity of Northern California; north relief depicts Christ teaching from Matthew 5 and 6, south relief depicts the resurrected Savior appearing to the Nephites from 3 Nephi 11 - churchofjesuschrist.org, newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org
[T2] 13th dedicated temple; 4770 Lincoln Ave; 18.1-acre site; reinforced concrete faced with Sierra white granite from Raymond, California; four instruction rooms, seven sealing rooms, one baptistry; first temple in the United States designed for film presentation of the endowment and the first with multiple instruction rooms feeding a central celestial room; cascading waterfall and creek-style water feature in the front gardens - churchofjesuschristtemples.org

CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Floor area - T1 Church News and newsroom both say approximately 95,000 sq ft, T2 says 80,157. Tier 1 wins and the approximate form shipped. The gap may be pre- versus post-renovation, but no source says so, so it was not asserted.
Relief panel scripture references - the almanac describes the panels generically, the Church's own media page cites Matthew 5-6 and 3 Nephi 11:8-20. The specific citation shipped.
