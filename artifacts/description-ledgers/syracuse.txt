CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Distance from Salt Lake City - Newsroom 2020 announcement summary said approximately 35 miles north; Newsroom 2021 groundbreaking release says about 30 miles northwest; Church News 2025 says 30 miles north of Temple Square. Used "about 30 miles northwest of Salt Lake City" per the groundbreaking release, the most specific Tier 1 wording.
Utah temple count - Newsroom open house article said it would be the 22nd OPERATING temple in Utah (Salt Lake and Provo Rock Canyon not operating); dedication coverage says 24th DEDICATED in Utah. Used the dedicated count and labeled it as such.
Floor area - rendering-stage figure was approximately 89,000 sq ft; final figure is 90,526 sq ft (Church News, Almanac, T2 all agree). Used 90,526 for the spec row and kept 89,000 only as the rendering-stage plan figure in the Construction Story.

VOLATILE (re-check before reprint)
206th dedicated temple / 24th dedicated in Utah - counts grow with each new dedication; true as of the June 8, 2025 dedication.
Temple district of 16 stakes - districts get redrawn as temples are added nearby.
First Temple President row - Val Dean Steel is the first president (historical, safe), but presidents rotate roughly every three years; do not describe him as current without rechecking.
"Third temple built in Davis County" - phrased as a build-order fact, which stays true, but more Davis County temples may exist by reprint time.
"First temple dedicated with two baptismal fonts" - the first claim is permanent, but later temples may also have two fonts; keep the phrasing "first to be dedicated with," never "only."
