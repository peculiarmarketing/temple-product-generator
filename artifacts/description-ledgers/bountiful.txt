CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Announcement date - Church News 2010 overview says "announced May 28, 1988"; almanac and T2 say February 2, 1990. Both are real events: 1988 was the land purchase "for the possible construction of a temple" (Deseret News 1990-03-03 recounts the sequence), 1990 was the decision letter. Published both, labeled.
Open house attendance - 870,361 (almanac, T2, precise count) vs "estimated 900,000 to 1.1 million" (Church News 2010). The precise official count wins; the 2010 estimate likely folds in unticketed visitors.
Open house start - T2 says November 4, almanac says public opening November 5 with leadership tour November 4. T1 wins; printed November 5.
Site acreage - 9 acres (T2, and the 1988 purchase description) vs 11 acres (Church News 2010). Unresolved two-way T1/T2 split; acreage omitted from the copy.
