SOURCE LEDGERS - the ten temples that blocked the 16 September 2026 catalogue run
Research date: September 16, 2026

Why these ten: the Tapstitch catalogue runner refuses a row whose temple has no
temple-facts.html, so 30 rows would not publish. Five of the ten turned out to
have researched facts already, published on their old Printify listings and
never saved back into the temple folder. Those were recovered rather than
re-researched. Five needed real research and are ledgered below.

================================================================================
RECOVERED, NOT RE-RESEARCHED
================================================================================

Logan, Provo, Kirtland, Cody and Taylorsville already carried a complete
<section class="temple-facts"> in the description of their old (now drafted)
Shopify products. Recovered by reading the live description, unwrapping the
collapsed <details> markup with description_html._unwrap, and writing the raw
fragment back to Temples/{Name}/temple-facts.html.

Each recovery was proved lossless by asserting
collapse_temple_facts(recovered) == the live section, byte for byte, before the
file was written. The h3 was matched per temple to avoid the Provo collision:
"Provo Utah Temple (1972-2024)" is the demolished 1972 building this product
depicts, NOT "Provo City Center Temple" and NOT the Rock Canyon replacement.

Tiering was done when that copy was first written and is not re-asserted here.
Nothing in the recovered text was edited.
