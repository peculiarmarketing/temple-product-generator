VERIFIED

Monson at general conference; groundbreaking Oct 23, 2010 with Monson presiding; open house Jan 14 - Feb 16, 2019 with about 52,000 visitors, first journalist tours led by Elder David A. Nelson in seven sessions, Nelson conducting the cornerstone ceremony on the first day - thechurchnews.com almanac, newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org

[T1] Nelson met Pope Francis at the Vatican on March 9, 2019, the first formal audience between a president of the Church and a pope - thechurchnews.com (March 9, 2019)
[T1] Dedication came nearly 170 years after Lorenzo Snow opened missionary work in Italy in 1850; Snow's "opened a door which no man can shut"; Snow's 1851-published account "The work here is slow and tedious. Nevertheless, the Church has been established. The tree has been planted and is spreading its roots." - thechurchnews.com (March 10, 2019)
[T1] Three stories, about 40,000 sq ft on a 15-acre site in northeast Rome including a meetinghouse, family history center and patron housing; floor-to-ceiling art glass at the entrance depicting a scene from the life of Christ, other windows drawn from the olive trees; olive trees on site 400-500 years old; architect Niels Valentiner; interior Perlato Svevo and Cenia marble, sapele, burl and cherry wood, Murano glass fixtures from Venice; design inspired by the Roman church San Carlino - thechurchnews.com almanac, thechurchnews.com (March 10, 2019)
[T2] 162nd dedicated temple; first in Italy; Via di Settebagni 376 beside the Grande Raccordo Anulare; Bianco Sardo granite facade over cast-in-place concrete; 156 ft 3 in; two attached end spires with angel Moroni; two instruction rooms, three sealing rooms, one baptistry; interior floor patterns after Michelangelo's Piazza del Campidoglio; angel Moroni installed March 25, 2017 - churchofjesuschristtemples.org

CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Floor area - T1 says about 40,000 sq ft, T2 says 41,010. Not a real disagreement; the T1 rounding shipped.
European ordinal - T1 almanac calls it the twelfth temple in Europe, T2 calls it the thirteenth. T2 counts dedicated temples including ones no longer operating as such. The T2 figure shipped since it matches the same source's numbering used elsewhere in the block, and the discrepancy is noted here.
Architect spelling - sources print both "Niels" and "Neils" Valentiner. "Niels" shipped.

VOLATILE (re-check before reprint)
"Thirteenth in Europe" - ordinals of this kind move as temples are dedicated, and the sources already disagree
Nothing else in this entry is time-sensitive; the temple is recently dedicated with no renovation history
