# Description Source Ledgers - 8 Utah Temples, 32 Products - 20 August 2026

Temples: Cedar City, Deseret Peak, Draper, Jordan River, Layton, Lindon,
Monticello, Mount Timpanogos. Each ledger covers all four garments, which
share one facts fragment.

Batch-wide notes:
- First Temple President omitted for the whole batch. Only Cedar City had one
  sourced (John W. Yardley, T1), and the almanac marks it current as of the
  2017 article rather than the first president, so it is volatile and it would
  have been the only entry carrying the row.
- Tier 2 (churchofjesuschristtemples.org) and Tier 1 disagree on floor area for
  two temples. Tier 1 won in both cases per the hierarchy. Both figures recorded
  below.
- Three temples print a city that differs from the temple name. Each states the
  reason in the Location row rather than leaving it to look like an error.
