CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Sealing room count - T2 says four sealing rooms; Church News almanac and the 2010 Church News entry say three. Tier 1 wins per hierarchy, published three. Re-check whether the 2011-2012 renovation changed the count; if the almanac is stale this is the likeliest error in the block.
Grant meeting year - T2 gives 1939 explicitly; Church News (jewel-of-treasure-valley) implies about 1938 ("some 25 years" after the 1913 stake) but names no year. Published 1939, the only explicit date on record.
Cultural celebration 9,200 figure - one Church News summary reads as audience attendance; the Newsroom rededication article states 9,200 youth participants. Published the Newsroom framing (performers).
1984 open house length - T2's data block says 18 days for May 1-19 while its own history text says 19-day open house. Published the dates only, no day count.
1987 baptistry date - Church News 2010 entry says the new baptistry was dedicated May 29, 1987; the almanac says it reopened June 2, 1987. Dropped the detail rather than pick one.

VOLATILE (re-check before reprint)
Ordinance room count - live conflict between current T1 and T2 sources, see above.
"Second built in Idaho" - anchored to build order and stable, but Idaho's temple count keeps growing; keep the phrasing anchored to build order, never "second in Idaho" unqualified.
"Most dedicatory sessions since Salt Lake" - true of 1984 and phrased past-anchored ("at the time"); Bountiful held 28 sessions in 1995, so never modernize the phrasing.
District figures (100,000 members, 31 stakes) - stated as of the 2012 rededication and will drift; the copy pins them to that date.
Temple number 27 - dedication order, fixed, but any restatement as "of N operating temples" would expire immediately.
