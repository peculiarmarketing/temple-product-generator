CONFLICTS RESOLVED
Exterior material - T1 almanac says lightly variegated gray granite, T2 spec says travertine. Not a contradiction once dated: granite is the 2004 facade, travertine is the current re-clad. Both shipped, each labeled to its era.
Announcing officer - neither T1 nor T2 names a person for the Aug 7, 2002 announcement, so no name shipped. Hinckley's role is carried by the sourced journal entry instead.
