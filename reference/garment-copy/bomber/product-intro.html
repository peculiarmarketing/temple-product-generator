From the Founder

The ring around this seal is 1 Peter 2:9, the verse this company is named after, all four phrases of it. Inside the ring is the Salt Lake Temple in the same line style as every temple in the shop.

Most of what we make hints at the name. I wanted one piece that says it outright, and I put it on a jacket because the back of a jacket is what people read when you walk past them in a hallway.
