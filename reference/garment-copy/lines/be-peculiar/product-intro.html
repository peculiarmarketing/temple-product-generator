From the Founder

This company is named after a verse most of us could finish from memory: a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people. In the King James Bible, peculiar means set apart. A people God keeps for His own.

Somewhere along the way the word drifted into meaning strange, and I'd like it back. The full verse is printed in small type under the big word.

Spanish needed a different line. Translate Be Peculiar word for word and the scripture disappears, so Sé Singular carries a verse the Spanish Bible already has, Deuteronomy 14:2 in the Reina-Valera: Jehová te ha escogido para que le seas un pueblo singular. The King James puts the same verse as "a peculiar people."

- Evan
